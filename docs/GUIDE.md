# Your fully funded master's roadmap

A plain-English guide for a BSCS student in Pakistan aiming for a fully funded AI/CS master's
abroad **without paying an agent**. ScholarRadar finds and tracks the opportunities; this guide
covers everything else. Dates are typical patterns: always confirm on official websites.

---

## 1. How the process works

```
Find scholarship -> Apply to university + scholarship (free, online)
      -> Admission letter + scholarship award letter
      -> Apply for student visa yourself (embassy / VFS / Gerry's)
      -> Travel -> Start studies
```

- **You don't need an agent.** Every step is online or at the embassy. Anyone asking money for a
  "guaranteed" scholarship is a red flag.
- **"Visa sponsorship" for students** means: the university's admission letter supports your
  visa, and the scholarship award letter is your **proof of funds**. That is why a fully funded
  scholarship makes "zero expense" possible (for example, it can replace Germany's blocked account).

## 2. IELTS or no IELTS?

You have two tracks, and ScholarRadar separates them in every email:

| Track | What you need | Examples (verify each year) |
|---|---|---|
| **No IELTS** | English Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from VU | Turkiye Burslari, GKS, CSC, MEXT, many Stipendium Hungaricum & Erasmus Mundus programmes |
| **IELTS/TOEFL** | IELTS Academic 6.5+ (or TOEFL / Duolingo where accepted) | KAUST, Chevening, UK universities, many German programmes |

Plan: **get the MOI letter now** (free/cheap). Take IELTS only when a scholarship you really
want requires it. Duolingo English Test is cheaper and accepted by a growing number of universities.

## 3. Documents: start collecting now

Run `python -m scholar_radar checklist` for the full list with why/how/when/cost. The core set:

1. Passport (valid for the whole study period)
2. Transcripts: HEC attested (HEDAS portal)
3. Degree certificate, or an expected-graduation letter from VU until you graduate
4. **MOI letter** from VU
5. CV (Europass format for Europe), with your AI/automation projects
6. Statement of purpose / motivation letter (one master draft, tailored per programme)
7. 2-3 recommendation letters. Ask your final-year-project supervisor early.
8. GitHub portfolio (this project counts!)
9. Later: police character certificate, medical certificate, HEC nomination (some scholarships)
10. Italy only: Family Registration Certificate + income documents for need-based DSU scholarships

## 5. Money you still need up front

Even with a fully funded scholarship, expect some costs **before** the scholarship starts paying:
passport, HEC attestation, optional English test, some university application fees (ScholarRadar
filters these out), visa fee, police/medical certificates, courier/translation, and sometimes the
flight. A rough total is **PKR 150,000-400,000** depending on country; plan and save early.

## 6. Work experience changes your options

With 0 years of experience, focus on: Erasmus Mundus, Stipendium Hungaricum, Turkiye Burslari,
GKS, CSC, MEXT, KAUST, MBZUAI and Italy DSU.

After about 2 years of work (freelance/remote work can count if documented), Chevening, DAAD EPOS
and the Swedish Institute scholarships open up. ScholarRadar lists them under **"For later"**.
Update `work_experience_years` in `config/profile.yaml` when that changes.

## 7. Using the weekly email

1. **Deadline alerts** first: act on anything under 14 days.
2. For each new **strong** match, open the official page and check the **"Verify"** items.
3. Add real targets to your own tracker (a simple Google Sheet: name, deadline, status, documents).
4. Use **"Documents you need"** to see which documents unlock the most scholarships.

## 8. Avoid scams

- Official scholarships never require paying an agent, "processing fee" or "guarantee fee".
- Apply only on official domains (`.gov`, `.edu`, `.ac`, university and embassy websites).
- Visa appointments are booked only through the official embassy / VFS / Gerry's websites.
- Double-check any deadline found through news sites or AI on the official page.
